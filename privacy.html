Privacy policy

This policy explains what Bahubali Enterprise, Bijoynagar, Assam, India does with the information you send through this website.

What we collect

We collect only what you type into the quotation and partnership forms: your name, business name, phone number, email address, location and the details of your requirement. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by phone, we receive the details you share there.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quotation
  • To discuss a possible order or partnership with you
  • To keep a record of our business correspondence

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Services that process your details

Form enquiries may be delivered to us through a form delivery service or through WhatsApp, and the map on this site is provided by Google Maps. These services handle data under their own privacy policies. This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details you sent by contacting us using the information on the contact section of this site.
