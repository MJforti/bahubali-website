FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Not answered here? Add your question to the quotation form and we will reply with your quote.

How do I request a quotation?

Fill in the quotation form on this page, send us a WhatsApp message, or call us. Include the products and quantities you need.

What should I include in my request?

Product names, sizes or preferred brands, quantities, your site or delivery location and the date you need the materials. The more detail you give, the more accurate the quotation.

Does it cost anything to request a quotation?

No. Requesting a quotation is free and you are under no obligation to place an order.

Do you deliver to project sites?

Supply arrangements depend on the order and your location. Mention your site location in your enquiry and we will confirm what is possible before you order.

Do you work with buyers outside Assam?

We welcome enquiries from businesses across India and from partners abroad. We will confirm what we can do for each request individually.

What does Bahubali Enterprise supply?

Hardware, building materials and basic construction requirements. The catalogue on this page shows the main areas we cover. Send us your list and we will confirm what we can supply.

Who do you work with?

Mainly business buyers: contractors, builders, construction companies, hardware retailers, wholesalers, distributors, procurement teams and commercial establishments.

Where are you located?

We are based in Bijoynagar, Assam, India. You will find the map and directions in the contact section below.

How can I become a distributor or partner?

Submit the partnership enquiry form with your business details and requirements. Our team will contact you to discuss the next step.
